# Vetted analyst stats for the landscape slide (graded, 2026-08-31)

## USE THESE — the six slide-worthy numbers, CFO phrasing included

1. **$13.50 vs $1.84** per contact, assisted vs self-service (Gartner,
   "Benchmarks to Assess Your Customer Service Costs") — replaces the stale
   2019 $8.01/$0.10. CFO line: "Every call a human answers costs ~7x a
   self-served contact — and voice is your biggest channel."
2. **~33% of handle time** goes to capturing identity + reason for calling;
   automating it upstream removes it (Gartner). CFO line: "A third of every
   call is spent finding out who's calling. Ember does that before hello."
3. **52% of DIY smart-home users hit setup/connectivity issues; 36% of
   returns cite setup, not hardware; 1 in 5 'very difficult' setups end in
   a return** (Parks Associates PR, Jul 2025 — primary). CFO line: "A third
   of your returns never had a broken product in the box."
4. **$30–65 processing cost per returned CE unit** (reverse-logistics
   benchmarks). CFO line: "One avoidable return costs more than three
   support calls."
5. **$80B agent-labor savings from conversational AI in 2026; 10% of
   interactions fully automated, up from 1.6%** (Gartner PR, Aug 2022 —
   primary; fine print: most value is partial containment + agent assist).
   Longer arc: **80% of common inquiries resolved agentically by 2029,
   −30% opex** (Gartner). CFO line: "The wave has a Gartner price tag."
6. **Sonos: 2–3x resolution rate with an AI agent** (Sierra deployment;
   vendor-published, customer-named — closest comp to Aria Home).
   Backups: ClassPass −95% support cost, Curology −65%, Flashfood >90%
   autonomous (all Decagon vendor case studies); Camping World +33% agent
   productivity (independently cited in Forrester Wave Q2 2025).

Churn tie: **+1 point CES ≈ +12–15% repurchase intent** (Effortless
Experience lineage — CEB/Gartner, surfaced via Decagon). Channel-switching
raises effort **2–3x**.
