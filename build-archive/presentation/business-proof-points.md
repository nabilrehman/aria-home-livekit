# Business Proof Points — for the panel (Ahmad + Varun especially)

Cited, real numbers to ground the Context and Value slides and to answer Ahmad (CX/ops) and Varun (finance). Frame as **industry benchmarks** in the room ("industry data shows… your actual numbers we'd prove in a pilot"), not promises.

## The problem is real & growing (smart-home specific) — for the Context slide
- US internet households average **16 connected devices**; **52%** had ≥1 technical problem last year, **38%** had two or more. → every device is latent support volume. (Parks Associates / IoT For All)
- **63%** of consumers still prefer to handle technical support **over the phone**. → voice is where the volume is. (Parks Associates)
- IoT devices to surpass **32.1 billion by 2030**; Gartner: **1 in 5** inbound service contacts from digital customers by 2026. → volume only grows.

## Deflection & resolution — for Ahmad
- Enterprise voice AI deflects **45–60%** of tier-1 calls (2026 median 41%, top quartile 59%). Routine intents (order status, resets, refunds) deflect **70%+**; nuanced complaints stay with humans (<25%). → automate the routine, route the hard.
- Best-in-class AI resolution (not just deflection): **~76% average**, **70–84%** for ecommerce. (Fin AI)
- Distinction to make: **deflection ≠ resolution.** We optimize for *resolved*, and escalate cleanly (with a summary) when not.

## Cost & ROI — for Varun
- A US support agent costs **$35–50K/yr** loaded; an AI-handled interaction costs **$0.50–$2.00**.
- Conversational AI cuts per-interaction cost **65–90%** — below **$1** per automated resolution.
- Worked example (use on the Value slide): **50,000 conversations/month**, shift **60%** to AI at ~$0.99 vs ~$8 human → **~$210K/month (~$2.5M/year)** saved.
- ROI: **$3.50 per $1** spent on average, up to **8x** for leaders. Gartner: conversational AI to cut contact-centre labour costs **$80B in 2026**.

## Named case studies — one line each (credibility)
- **PAL Airlines:** wait time to **under a minute**, monthly CS cost **down ~30%**.
- **Bangkok electronics retailer** (35 agents, voice bot): **86%** wait-time reduction, **74%** fewer abandons, **+45% CSAT**.
- **McKinsey:** an energy co. shaved **60 seconds** off caller authentication alone with a voice assistant.

## LiveKit credibility (why-us, for Mike + everyone)
- **OpenAI runs ChatGPT Advanced Voice Mode on LiveKit** — millions of voice conversations daily. Same stack under **Character.AI, Spotify, Reddit**.
- OpenAI + LiveKit **partnered** to expose Advanced Voice as an API. LiveKit reached a **$1B valuation** (Jan 2026).
- Scale proof: one 16-core SFU handles **1 publisher → 3,000 subscribers @ 92% CPU**; telephony stack handles **thousands of concurrent calls**. → "the infrastructure under the biggest voice product in the world is the same one under your support line."
