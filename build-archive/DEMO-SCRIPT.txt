---------------------------------------------------------------------

  Click "Call as a guest" on the store.

  YOU: "Hi, I need help with my account."
  EMBER: asks for phone number or account number.
      say-to-panel: "No caller ID on the web — so she asks, exactly
      as the brief requires when the phone isn't used. She has two
      tools right now; the rest don't exist until she knows who's
      calling. On a real SIP call the trunk hands us the number and
      this whole exchange disappears."

  YOU: "My phone number is five one two, five five five, one one
       eight eight."
  EMBER: reads it back, then: "Hi Sarah... I can see your account."
      say-to-panel: "Lookup by phone — the assignment's primary path.
      The account-number path is identical: last-ten-digits match on
      Cloud SQL through Google's MCP Toolbox, so 'AH 4821', '4821',
      any spacing all resolve. Try it later if you like."

  YOU: "What's the status of order five eight one two one?"
  EMBER: Indoor Camera two pack, processing, delivers September third.

  YOU: "And order five eight one three one?"
  EMBER: "No order with that number on this account."
      say-to-panel (Mike): "Same tool, same second — that one is
      Nabil's. The database won't return it: parameterized secure
      views, a role with no base-table access, and the account never
      comes from the model."

  YOU: "Okay, bye."  → she says goodbye, call ends.

CALL 2 — NABIL, SIGNED IN (requirements 1a, 2, 3, 4)        ~5.5 min
  Switch to Tab 1, click "Start call with Ember".

  Act 1 · Greeting + preload (60s)
  EMBER (unprompted): "Hi Nabil, I can see your account..."
      say-to-panel (Ahmad): "Firebase verified him; the account rides

  EMBER: it's not reporting — needs a battery... and a note that your
      dog Biscuit sets it off.
      say-to-panel (Mike): "Two stores in one answer: live telemetry
      from Firestore and a fact from Vertex AI Memory Bank — both
      preloaded in parallel with the call setup. Zero tool calls."

  Act 2 · The smart-home questions (90s)  — requirement 2
  YOU: "What's the status of my most recent order?"
  EMBER: order five eight one three one, processing, early September.
      say-to-panel (Varun): "That's a plain REST call into an order

  EMBER: on, ~sixty-nine degrees, cooling.
      say-to-panel: "Registry in Postgres, live state in Firestore,
      joined on device id."

  EMBER: fourteen days — security devices have a shorter window.
      say-to-panel: "That nuance — 30 days normally, 14 for security

  Bring the desk tab into view FIRST. Give the caveat line now:

  EMBER: takes the details (which order, what's wrong), then: "One

  DESK: rings — Nabil · refunds · summary · mood · urgency · next
      steps, before anyone accepts.
      say-to-panel (Ahmad): "Read the screen before you click. This

      And the refund decision was made by code from the policy dates,
      not by the model."
  Click ACCEPT (as Ahmad). Ember introduces the specialist and goes
      silent. YOU (as Ahmad): "Hi Nabil, I've got the damaged order

  Click END CALL. The case summary stays under Recent on the desk.
      say-to-panel (Varun): "Every transferred call becomes a masked
      record in Firestore — brief, outcome, transcript. A QA dataset
      for free."

  Epilogue (20s, optional)  — memory across calls
  Start a new call: "Anything I told you about my back door?"
  EMBER: "Your back door lock sticks when it's cold."
      say-to-panel: "Same customer, new session. That's the
      difference between a bot and a support line."
